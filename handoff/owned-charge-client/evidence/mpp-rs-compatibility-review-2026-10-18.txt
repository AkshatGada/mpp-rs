FINAL DESIGN AND SCOPE
ChargeClient owns a private reqwest client, accepts only ClientBuilder, and
applies Policy::none() last. Both initial and paid requests execute through that
retained client even when supplied a builder from a different client.
Valid request-level Accept-Payment is required; opaque defaults cannot authorize
payment. Invalid, repeated and all-zero values fail closed. Charge-only offers
are filtered before preparation. Selected/prepared offers are rechecked for
transport limits, provider support, request preferences and expiry.
The shared flow retains ranking, fallback, body cloning and retry limits.
API Authorization survives custom-header payment. Redirects are returned without
following Location. No session management transport is supplied, and no session,
top-up, voucher or close API is added. Legacy guards remain.
